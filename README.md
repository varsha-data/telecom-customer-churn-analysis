# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes telecom customer data to identify factors related with customer churn and generate business insights for customer retention.

## Objective
The objective is to understand customer churn patterns across contract types, tenure, monthly charges, internet services, payment methods, and customer characteristics.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset
The project uses the Telco Customer Churn dataset containing customer demographic, service, contract, payment, and billing information.

Dataset source: Kaggle — Telco Customer Churn

- Tenure analysis

- Business insight generation

## Key Findings
- The overall customer churn rate was approximately 26.5%.
- Month-to-month contract customers had the highest churn rate.
- Customers who churned had substantially lower average tenure than customers who stayed.
- Churned customers had higher average monthly charges.
- Fiber optic customers showed the highest churn rate among internet service categories.
- Electronic check customers had the highest churn rate among payment methods.
- Customers without partners or dependents showed higher churn rates.

## Business Recommendations
- Encourage month-to-month customers to adopt longer-term contracts through targeted offers.
- Develop stronger onboarding and engagement strategies for newer customers.
- Review pricing and perceived value for customers with higher monthly charges.
- Investigate service experience among fiber optic customers.
- Evaluate the electronic check payment experience and encourage alternative payment methods where appropriate.
- Develop targeted retention campaigns for higher-risk customer segments.
